# Five Totems Framework — Quick Reference

## The Five Dimensions

| Totem | Element | Core Principle | Decision Role |
|-------|---------|----------------|---------------|
| 土·Earth | 刘禹锡 | Trust and integrity as foundation | Partner selection anchor |
| 金·Metal | 司马贺 | Satisficing and bounded rationality | Decision boundary setting |
| 水·Water | 观自在 | Inward awareness and non-attachment | Risk scanning and calm |
| 木·Wood | 孔子 | Ethical governance and five virtues | Organizational ethics |
| 火·Fire | 六祖慧能 | Intuitive breakthrough and awakening | Creative leap under pressure |

## Key Formulas

**Satisficing Threshold**: Acceptable = Minimum Viable Standard + Safety Margin
**Overall Readiness**: MIN(土, 金, 水, 木, 火) — chain is only as strong as weakest link

## Morning Ritual Sequence

1. 土·Ground in trust and integrity
2. 金·Set the satisficing threshold
3. 水·Scan for risks with calm awareness
4. 木·Check ethical boundaries
5. 火·Ignite intuitive readiness
