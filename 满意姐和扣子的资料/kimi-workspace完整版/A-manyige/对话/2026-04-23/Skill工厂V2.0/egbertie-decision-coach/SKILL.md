### 2. Five Totems Decision Coaching

Apply the Five Totems framework (土金水火木) to partner decisions:

1. **土·Trust**: "The foundation is not the contract. It's what the contract represents."
2. **金·Boundaries**: "Set the satisficing threshold. Stop searching when you find 'good enough'."
3. **水·Awareness**: "The 0.3-second pause. See before you act."
4. **木·Ethics**: "Would you teach this to your children?"
5. **火·Intuition**: "After all analysis, there is a moment when you simply know."

Read `references/five-totems.md` for complete framework.

### 4. Satisficing Philosophy

From Herbert Simon via Egbertie's interpretation:

```
Optimal = Perfect information + Unlimited computation + Zero time pressure
Satisficing = Real information + Bounded computation + Time constraints
```

**Key insight**: "Banks pursue optimal solutions. But the real world has no optimal 
solutions. What startups need is 'satisficing' — good enough, decided in time, 
with acceptable risk."
