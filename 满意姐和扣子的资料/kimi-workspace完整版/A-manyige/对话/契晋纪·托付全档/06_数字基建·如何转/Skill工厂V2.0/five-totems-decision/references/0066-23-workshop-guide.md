# Five Totems Workshop Guide

## Workshop Format: "The Partner Decision Journey"

**Duration**: Half-day (4 hours) or Full-day (8 hours)
**Participants**: Founders + potential partners (2-6 people)
**Materials**: Five Totem cards, decision worksheet, physical anchors

---

## Opening (30 min)

### Hook Story

Begin with a real story:

> "It was 11 PM. Another founder messaged me: 'My co-founder hasn't spoken to me in three days. We have a board meeting tomorrow. What do I do?' This is not unusual. Partner conflict is the hidden cancer of startups. Today, we give you a language and a framework to prevent this — or navigate it when it happens."

### Ground Rules

1. What happens here stays here (confidentiality)
2. No "winning" — only "understanding"
3. Speak from experience, not theory
4. The framework is a tool, not a truth

---

## Module 1: 土·Trust (Earth) — 45 min

### Physical Anchor
A stone or soil sample. Pass it around. Feel its weight.

### Facilitation Script

> "This stone is older than your company. It has seen partnerships form and dissolve. What makes a partnership last is not the contract — it's what the contract represents. Trust is not a feeling. It is a pattern of consistent behavior over time."

### Exercise: Trust Timeline

Each participant draws a timeline of their most important partnership. Mark:
- When trust was highest
- When trust was tested
- What rebuilt it (or didn't)

### Key Question
> "If you had to describe your current partnership in one metaphor, what would it be? A bridge? A garden? A fortress?"

---

## Module 2: 金·Boundaries (Metal) — 45 min

### Physical Anchor
A ruler or measuring tape. Show how it has clear markings.

### Facilitation Script

> "Herbert Simon won a Nobel Prize for proving that humans cannot optimize — we satisfice. We find 'good enough' and move on. In partnerships, this means: define your minimum viable standard. Not the perfect partner. The partner who is good enough."

### Exercise: Satisficing Threshold

Write down:
1. Absolute must-haves (non-negotiable)
2. Nice-to-haves (negotiable)
3. Deal-breakers

Compare with partner. Where do thresholds align? Where do they diverge?

### Key Question
> "What would 'good enough' look like in this partnership? Where would you stop searching?"

---

## Module 3: 水·Awareness (Water) — 45 min

### Physical Anchor
A bowl of water. Disturb it. Watch it settle.

### Facilitation Script

> "Water takes the shape of its container without losing its nature. In decision-making, awareness is not control — it is seeing clearly what is, without forcing it to be something else. The 0.3-second pause: before reacting, breathe. See. Then act."

### Exercise: 0.3-Second Pause Practice

Partners sit facing each other. One shares a frustration. The other practices:
1. Listen fully (no interrupting)
2. Pause (count 0.3 seconds)
3. Respond from observation, not reaction

### Key Question
> "What are you not seeing in this partnership? What is the water showing you?"

---

## Module 4: 木·Ethics (Wood) — 45 min

### Physical Anchor
A small plant or seed. Growth requires structure and nurture.

### Facilitation Script

> "Confucius said: 导之以德，齐之以礼. Lead with virtue, align with ritual. In partnerships, ethics is not a constraint — it is the trellis on which growth happens. Without it, the vine collapses."

### Exercise: Five Virtues Assessment

Rate the partnership on Confucian five virtues (1-10):
- 仁 (Benevolence)
- 义 (Righteousness)
- 礼 (Propriety)
- 智 (Wisdom)
- 信 (Trustworthiness)

Discuss: Which virtue is strongest? Which needs cultivation?

### Key Question
> "If your children were to inherit this partnership's culture, what would you want them to learn from it?"

---

## Module 5: 火·Intuition (Fire) — 45 min

### Physical Anchor
A candle. Light it. Watch the flame move.

### Facilitation Script

> "Huineng said: 不立文字，直指人心. Do not rely on words; point directly to the human heart. After all the analysis, after all the frameworks, there is a moment when you simply know. This is not irrational — it is the integration of everything you have experienced."

### Exercise: Intuitive Check

Partners close their eyes. Each asks silently:
> "If I knew the answer, what would it be?"

Then share. Compare intuitive knowing with analytical conclusion.

### Key Question
> "What is your gut telling you about this partnership? What are you afraid to admit?"

---

## Closing: 契·晋 Commitment (30 min)

### 契 (Covenant)

Partners write a "契" — a one-sentence commitment to each other:
> "I commit to [specific action] in this partnership."

Sign it. Exchange.

### 晋 (Advancement)

Partners write a "晋" — a one-sentence growth vow:
> "I will grow in [specific dimension] through this partnership."

Plant the seed (physical anchor from 木 module) together.

---

## Card Game Rules

### Setup
- 5 Totem cards (土金水火木)
- 13 Conflict cards (Type 01-13)
- 1 Decision scenario card (drawn randomly)

### Play (3-5 players, 30-60 min)

1. **Draw Scenario**: A realistic partnership situation
2. **Diagnose**: Identify which conflict type(s) apply
3. **Map Totems**: Place affected totem cards on the scenario
4. **Intervene**: Each player proposes an intervention using one totem
5. **Vote**: Best intervention wins the round
6. **Rotate**: New scenario, new judge

### Advanced Mode
- Combine 2 conflict types (common in reality)
- Require intervention using 2+ totems
- Add "external pressure" cards that shift totem weights

## Physical Anchor Guide

| Totem | Anchor | Symbolism | Sourcing |
|-------|--------|-----------|----------|
| 土 | Stone/soil sample | Foundation, permanence | Garden stone or soil from meaningful place |
| 金 | Ruler | Measurement, boundaries | Any ruler; ideally vintage or engraved |
| 水 | Bowl of water | Adaptability, clarity | Glass bowl; change water each session |
| 木 | Seed or small plant | Growth, structure | Bonsai or seedling; let participants plant |
| 火 | Candle | Illumination, transformation | Scented candle; light at opening, extinguish at close |

---

## Facilitator Checklist

Before workshop:
- [ ] All physical anchors prepared
- [ ] Scenario cards printed/shuffled
- [ ] Worksheet copies printed
- [ ] Room arranged in circle (no tables as barriers)
- [ ] Confidentiality agreement signed

During workshop:
- [ ] Timekeeping strict (use timer)
- [ ] No one dominates; ensure equal voice
- [ ] Redirect theory-talk to experience-sharing
- [ ] Watch for emotional escalation; pause if needed

After workshop:
- [ ] Collect worksheets (optional: keep for follow-up)
- [ ] Send summary within 24 hours
- [ ] Offer 1:1 follow-up for pairs who identified 🔴 issues
- [ ] Plant tracking: check on 木 seed after 30 days
