## 2. Core Theory

### 2.1 Satisficing Principle (满意解)

From Herbert Simon's bounded rationality:

```
Optimal Solution = Requires perfect information + unlimited computation
Satisficing Solution = "Good enough" given real-world constraints
```

**Decision rule**: Set an aspiration level. Stop searching when the first alternative 
meeting that level is found. This is not settling — it is rational under uncertainty.

### 2.2 Five-Dimensional Assessment

When evaluating any partnership or decision:

**Step 1**: Score each dimension 1-10
**Step 2**: Overall Readiness = MIN(土, 金, 水, 木, 火)
**Step 3**: Identify the weakest dimension — this is the constraint
**Step 4**: Apply the corresponding totem's wisdom to strengthen

**Why MIN, not average**: A chain is only as strong as its weakest link. A partnership 
with 土=3 (trust breach) cannot be saved by 金=9 (perfect contract).

### 2.3 Dimension Interactions

| Conflict Pair | Nature | Resolution |
|--------------|--------|------------|
| 金·boundaries vs 火·intuition | Structure vs spontaneity | Set flexible boundaries that allow intuitive space |
| 土·trust vs 水·detachment | Attachment vs non-attachment | Trust deeply but hold loosely |
| 木·ethics vs 金·efficiency | Principles vs pragmatism | Ethics is the constraint, not efficiency |
| 火·breakthrough vs 水·calm | Action vs stillness | Calm is the foundation for true breakthrough |

## 3. Scoring Methodology

### 3.1 Dimension Scoring Guide

| Score | 土·Trust | 金·Boundaries | 水·Awareness | 木·Ethics | 火·Intuition |
|-------|----------|---------------|-------------|----------|-------------|
| 1-3 | Betrayal suspected | No contracts | Chaotic emotions | Ethics violated | Blocked/numb |
| 4-6 | Trust building | Basic agreements | Intermittent awareness | Gray zones | Occasional hunches |
| 7-8 | Established trust | Clear roles | Regular reflection | Strong values | Reliable gut feelings |
| 9-10 | Unconditional trust | Elegant systems | Continuous mindfulness | Ethical excellence | Synchronized intuition |

### 3.2 Interpretation

| Overall | Status | Action |
|---------|--------|--------|
| MIN ≥ 7 | 🟢 Ready to proceed | Monitor weakest dimension |
| MIN 4-6 | 🟡 Proceed with caution | Strengthen weakest dimension first |
| MIN ≤ 3 | 🔴 Do not proceed | Fix fundamental issues before any commitment |

## 4. Morning Ritual Sequence

For decision preparation or daily grounding:

1. **土·刘禹锡** — Ground in trust and integrity
   - Ask: "Is my foundation solid? Am I acting from integrity?"
   - Quote: "斯是陋室，惟吾德馨"

2. **金·司马贺** — Set the satisficing threshold
   - Ask: "What is 'good enough'? Where do I stop searching?"
   - Quote: "满意解优于最优解"

3. **水·观自在** — Scan for risks with calm awareness
   - Ask: "What am I not seeing? Where is the quiet before the storm?"
   - Quote: "向内观，不执于形"

4. **木·孔子** — Check ethical boundaries
   - Ask: "Does this honor 仁义礼智信? Would I teach this to others?"
   - Quote: "己所不欲，勿施于人"

5. **火·六祖慧能** — Ignite intuitive readiness
   - Ask: "What does my gut say? Where is the creative spark?"
   - Quote: "不立文字，直指人心"
